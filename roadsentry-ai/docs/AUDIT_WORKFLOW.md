# RoadSentry AI — 5C Infrastructure Audit Methodology

RoadSentry AI implements the internationally recognized **5C Audit Finding Framework**, aligned with INTOSAI standards and the Royal Audit Authority (RAA) of Bhutan:

## The 5C Framework Components

1. **Criteria (What should be):**
   - The established standard, specification, contract specification, or maintenance threshold (e.g. DoST Road Maintenance Standards, IRC:SP:72, FIDIC Red Book clauses).
2. **Condition (What is):**
   - The factual situation observed on the ground, corroborated by computer vision detections, bounding boxes, and photographic evidence.
3. **Cause (Why it occurred):**
   - The root reason for the variance.
   - *Mandatory Safeguard:* Unless substantiated by maintenance logs or contractor correspondence, RoadSentry AI labels this as `"Not established — requires auditor verification"`.
4. **Consequence / Effect (The Impact):**
   - Potential road user safety hazards, increased vehicle operating costs, accelerated structural subbase saturation, or compounding public reconstruction expenditure.
5. **Corrective Action / Recommendation (What should be done):**
   - Actionable remediation proposed to the executive agency (e.g., crack sealing, full-depth patch repair, drainage unclogging, contractor defect liability claim).

## Human-in-the-Loop Review Controls
- Auditors retain full editorial authority:
  - **Confirm Observation:** Marks finding as verified by lead auditor.
  - **Edit Finding:** Allows modifying wording, criteria citations, and recommendations.
  - **Mark Unverified:** Explicitly flags observation for on-site physical field core audit.
  - **Exclude Finding:** Drops finding from the draft audit dossier.
